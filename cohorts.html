PolypAtlas — CRSwNP Multi-omics Resource

Cohorts & datasets

Research use only: This site is an integrated query resource for publicly available chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps (CRSwNP) transcriptomic data, for research reference only. It does not provide clinical diagnosis, disease probability estimates, or treatment recommendations. All values come from uniform reanalysis of public data; known limitations of each cohort are annotated alongside the data.

Spatial datasets (1)

GSE235714 GeoMx DSP: 40 ROIs × 10 donors (CRSwNP 6 / controls 4); panel of 1,480 genes (Nanostring DSP Human CTA; measured, not whole-transcriptome).

Donor-level differential analysis finds zero FDR<0.05 genes in either compartment — the spatial layer is a compartment-localization/display layer, not an independent evidence layer for disease differences; only descriptive compartment logFC values are shown, with no concordance claims.
Disease is fully confounded with tissue: all CRSwNP donors are polyp tissue, all controls are ethmoid tissue.
GSM7508390 (CRSwNP_8 CD45+ ROI2) flagged ‘Low Surface Area’ in QC (retained, not removed).
Panel coverage: only 14.6% of the union of bulk top-100 genes is in the panel; genes not in the panel (e.g. CLC/CCL26/POSTN/ALOX15) explicitly return ‘not in the CTA panel’.
